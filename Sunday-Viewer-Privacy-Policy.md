# Privacy Policy for Sunday Viewer

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Sunday Viewer does not collect, sell, or use personal information for advertising or tracking.

The app uses your Microsoft account to access authorized Microsoft 365 and SharePoint content. Authentication and these requests are handled by Microsoft.

Sunday Viewer stores app settings and downloaded content on your device to provide its features and offline access. The developer does not receive this information.

The app also contacts the ESV API when you request Bible passages. No advertising or analytics services are used.
